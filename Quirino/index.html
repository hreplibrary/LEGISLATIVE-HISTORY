HISTORY

QUIRINO

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

The province of Quirino lies closely on Mamparam and Palae ranges in the Sierra Madre mountains, bounded by the Provinces of Isabela on the north, Aurora on the east and south, and Nueva Vizcaya on the west. A province with flat and rolling topography and crisscrossed by Addalan, Canwap and Diduyan rivers.

Quirino has a land area of 3,486.2 square kilometers. In 2007, it had a population of 163,610.

Following is a timeline1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Quirino:

DATE

EVENT
  • 1896

Outbreak of the Philippine Revolution.
  • 1898

Colonel Daniel Tirona established a revolutionary government in Northern Luzon.
  • September 15, 1898

The Filipino revolutionary headquarters received the notice of surrender of the Spanish forces of in Nueva Vizcaya through Major Delfin Esquizel, commander of the revolutionary forces in the area.
  • November, 1899

Cagayan Valley surrendered to the American forces led by Captain Joseph B. Batchelder.
  • December, 1941

Following the outbreak of World War II, guerrilla forces were organized under the command of Major Russell Volkmann of the USFIP-NL. The 14th Infantry under Major Romulo Manriquez garrisoned in Nueva Vizcaya.
  • 1951

Luis Perez Dasmarinas explored the Quirino region. Subsequently, the Dominicans founded several Christian settlements in the area that later became Cagayan and Nueva Vizcaya.
  • 1966

A bill was proposed by Congressman Leonardo B. Perez to create the subprovince of Quirino in honor of the late president of the Republic, Elpidio Quirino due to the great distances of Diffun, Saguday, Aglipay and Madella.
  • June 18, 1966

The proposed bill became Republic Act (RA) No. 4734.
  • June 21, 1969

RA 5554 provided for the segregation of Quirino Subprovince from Nueva Vizcaya.
  • September 10, 1971

Pursuant to RA 6394, Quirino was created as a province. This is thru the bill sponsored a bill that would separate Quirino from Nueva Vizcaya and give it an independent political status.
  • February 25, 1983
 The municipality of Nagtipunan in  the  province of Qurino was created by virtue of Batas Pambansa 345
  • As of 2013

Municipalities of Aglipay, Cabarroguis, Diffun, Maddela, Nagtipunan and Saguday.
