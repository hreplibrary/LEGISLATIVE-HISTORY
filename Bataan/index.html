HISTORY

BATAAN

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

Bataan is located at the southwest coast of Luzon, across Manila Bay from the city of Manila. Bataan faces Manila Bay on the east and China Sea on the west.

Bataan has a land area of 1,373 square kilometers. In 2007, it had a population of 662,153. At present,   Bataan is comprised of twelve municipalities with its capital at Balanga.

Following is a timeline1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Bataan:

DATE

EVENT
  • 1572

Spanish conqueror, Juan de Salcedo visited the area.
  • 1588

Abucay mission was founded.
  • 1596

Samal mission was founded.
  • 1667

Orion mission was founded.
  • 1714

Orani and Balanga missions were founded.
  • 1754

Bataan was founded as a province consisting of towns of Balanga, Abucay, Samal, Orani, Llana-Hermosa, San Juan de Dinalupijan, Pilar and Orion.
  • 1757

La Llana Hermosa mission was founded.
  • 1801

Pilar parish was founded.
  • March 2, 1901

A civil provincial government was established.
  • 1902
 By virtue of ACT NO. 548, a local civil government for the Negritos in Bataan was established.
  • 1903

Twelve municipalities of then existing in Bataan were reduced to eight by consolidating Mabatan(g) with Abuday (Abucay), Pilar with Balanga, Hermosa with Dinalupijan and Samal with Orani. Later, Pilar, Hermosa and Samal were re-established as separate municipalities again.

  • June 10, 1955

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 1249, the Municipality of Moron was changed to Municipality of Morong.
  • 1963

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 3762, Alañgan in the Municipality of Limay, Bataan became a duly constituted barrio.
  • 1965
 By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 4321, Kapunitan in the Municipality of Orion, Bataan became a duly constituted barrio.
  • As of 2013

Bataan constitutes two legislatives districts. Listed below are the municipalities that comprise the districts:
1st District: Municipalities of Abucay, Dinalupihan, Hermosa, Morong, Orani, and Samal.
2nd District: Municipalities of Bagac, Limay, Mariveles, Orion, Pilar, and Balanga City.
