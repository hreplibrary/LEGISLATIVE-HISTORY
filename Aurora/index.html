HISTORY

AURORA

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

Aurora is located at the northern part of Quezon Province. It is bounded on the north by Isabela province, on the south by the northern part of Quezon, on the east by the Pacific Ocean and on the west by Quirino, Nueva Vizcaya and Nueva Ecija.

Aurora has a land area of 3,147.32 square kilometers. In 2007, it had a population of 187,802.

Following is a timeline1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Aurora:

DATE

EVENT
  • 1571

Aurora was first explored by Juan de Salcedo when he led and expedition from Laguna to Paracale (Camarines Norte).

  • 1591

Quezon (then named Kalilaya) was organized into a district which included the provinces of Laguna and Nueva Ecija.
  • 1702

Nueva Ecija was split off from Kalilaya.
  • 1749

The provincial capital was transferred to Tayabas.
  • 1855-1885

Aurora, then named Principe, was made a comandancia politico-militar with its capital at Baler.
  • 1898

A provincial revolutionary government was established in Principe. Bondo was made a separate municipality.
  • 1951

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 648, Bondo district, which was enlarged and named Aurora to honor President Quezon’s visit in the place during the Commonwealth period, was created as a subprovince of Quezon.

  • June 16, 1956

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 1536, the Municipal District of Dingalan in the Municipality of Baler, subprovince of Aurora, Province of Quezon was created.
  • June 18, 1962

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 3487, the Municipal District of San Luis, subprovince of Aurora, Province of Quezon was converted into a municipality.
  • June 18, 1966

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 4785, the Municipal District of Dilasag, subprovince of Aurora, Province of Quezon was converted into a municipality.
  • November 21, 1978

By virtue of BATAS PAMBANSA  7, the subprovince of Aurora was separated from the province of Quezon and established as an independent province.
  • As of 2013

Aurora constitutes a lone district that includes the municipalities of Baler, Casiguran, Dilasag, Dinalungan, Dingalan, Dipaculao, Maria Aurora, and San Luis.
