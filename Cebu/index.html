HISTORY

CEBU

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

 The province of Cebu is located near the center of the Visayan region of the central Philippines.  It is separated from Bohol Island on the southeast by the Bohol Strait, from Negros Island on the west by the Tañon Strait, and from Leyte Island on the northeast by the Camotes Sea.

Cebu has a land area of 4,932.79 square kilometers. In 2007, it had a population of 2,439,005.  

Following is a timeline 1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Cebu:

EVENT
  • April 7, 1521

Ferdinand Magellan set foot at the island of Cebu. After how many days, Rajah Humabon, his wife and about 800 men were baptized into Christianity.
  • April 27, 1565

Miguel Lopez de Legazpi landed in the area and named it San Miguel.
  • January 1, 1571

Legazpi founded the present City of Cebu, changing the name of San Miguel to “La Villa del Santissimo de Jesus.”
  • 1580

Center of Christianity was established in Bantayan.
  • 1598

Center of Christianity was established in Cebu.
  • 1599

Center of Christianity was established in Carcar.
  • 1606

Center of Christianity was established in Boljo-on.
  • 1614

Center of Christianity was established in Barili.
  • 1738

Center of Christianity was established in Mandaue.
  • 1854

Bohol became an independent province.  In addition, Davao, Surigao, Agusan, Misamis, Lanao and Zamboanga provinces of Mindanao Island all had early administrative ties to Cebu.

  • 1863

The port of Cebu was opened to international trade, beginning the establishment of this city as a major economic center for this region.
  • 1898

The Spanish capitulated and evacuated from Cebu.
  • 1899

The revolutionary government was displaced by the Americans.
  • April 18, 1901

A civil government was established to replace the military government.
  • July 17, 1901

A military government was re-established in the province.
  • January 1, 1902

Civil provincial government was restored and the ports reopened.

  • 1903

Eleven of the municipalities have been reorganized and five cities have been chartered.
  • October 22, 1903

Under the Philippine Commission Act 952, the Camotes island group, formerly of Leyte province, was annexed to Cebu province.
  • October 27, 1903

The 57 municipalities of Cebu were reduced to 41 by consolidating Madridejos and Santa Fe with Bantayan, Sogod with Catmon, El Pardo and Talamban (formerly Mabolo) with Cebu, Alcoy with Dalaguete, Ronda with Dumanjug, Compostela with Liloan, Consolacion with Mandaue, Alcantara with Moalboal, Cordova and Santa Rosa with Opon. Nueva Caceres and Santander with Oslob, San Sebastian with Samboan, and Poro with Tudela.
  • November 14, 1935

The four barrios in the municipality of Toledo were created.
  • 1936

Cebu City was chartered.
  • June 21, 1959

By virtue of Republic Act (RA) 2603, the Barrio Dapdap in the municipality of San Remigio was created.
  • 1960

Toledo City was chartered.
  • June 18, 1960

By virtue of RA 2672, the charter of the city of Cebu was amended.
  • June 19, 1960

By virtue of RA 2771, the barrios of Bug-o and Molobolo in the municipalities of Asturias and Tuburan, respectively in the province were created.

By virtue of RA 2876, the barrio of Looc Norte in the municipality of Asturias was created.

By virtue of RA 2900, the barrio of Kambuhawi in the municipality of Balamban was created.
  • 1961

Danao City was chartered.

Lapu-Lapu City (formerly Opon municipality on Mactan Island, also known as “Mactan” was chartered.
  • June 18, 1961

By virtue of RA 3376, the barrio of Luy-a and Dayhagon in the municipality of Medellin was created.

By virtue of RA 3387, the barrio of Curva in the municipality of Medellin was created.

By virtue of RA 3388, the barrio of Daligdigan in the municipality of Tuburan was created.
  • June 21, 1963

By virtue of RA 3569, the first congressional district of the province was constituted into a separate engineering district.
  • 1969

Mandaue City was chartered.
  • September 18, 2015
 By virtue of RA 10684, the seventh congressional district of the province was created.
  • As of 2016

Cebu constitutes six legislative districts. Listed below are the municipalities that comprise the districts:
1st District: Municipalities of Carcar, Minglanilla, Naga, San Fernando, Sibonga and Talisay City.
2nd District: Municipalities of Alcoy, Argao, Boljoon, Dalaguete, Oslob, Santander, and Samboan.
3rd District: Municipalities of Aloguinsan, Asturias, Balamban, Barili, Pinamungajan, Tuburan, and Toledo City.
4th District: Municipalities of Bantayan, Bogo, Daanbantayan, Madridejos, Medellin, San Remigio, Santa Fe, Tabogon, Tabuelan.
5th District: Municipalities of Borbon, Carmen, Catmon, Compostela, Liloan, Pilar, Poro, San Francisco, Sogod, Tudela, and Danao City.
6th District: Municipalities of Consolacion and Cordova, Mandaue City, and Lapu-lapu City
7th District:Municipalities of Dumanjug, Ronda, Alcantara, Moalboal, Badian, Alegria, Malabuyoc, and Ginatilan.
