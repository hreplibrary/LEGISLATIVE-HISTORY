HISTORY

BOHOL

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

Bohol is located in the Southern Visayas region, in the south-central portion of the Philippines. It is bounded Southern Leyte on the east, and the island of Cebu on the west.

Bohol has a land area of 4,820.95 square kilometers and in 2007, it had a population of 1,230,110.  

Following is a timeline 1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Bohol:

DATE

EVENT
  • 1565

Miguel Lopez de Legazpi dropped anchor at Jagna, Bohol and later at the little town of Bo-ol (Bohol) where he entered blood compact with Datu Sikatuna.
  • 1595

The first Jesuit mission was established in Baclayon.
  • 1602

Jesuit mission was established in Loboc.
  • 1631

Jesuit mission was established in Jagna.
  • 1697

Jesuit mission was established in Dauis.
  • 1724

Jesuit mission was established in Talibon.
  • 1744

Second rebellion in Bohol arose led by Dagohoy.

  • 1767

Jesuit mission was established in Tagbilaran.
  • 1768

Jesuit mission was established in Maribojoc.

  • 1854

Bohol was separate from Cebu and, together with the island of Siquijor, became a separate politico-militar province, which remained such until the end of the Spanish rule in the Philippines.
  • 1896

A short-lived revolutionary government was established under Pedro Samson after the Spaniards evacuated Tagbilaran.
  • April 20, 1901

A provincial civil government was established after Tagbilaran was occupied by the Americans.
  • June 4, 1901

Siquijor was separated from Bohol and made a sub-province of Negros Oriental.
  • April 1, 1902

The civil government was re-established.
  • June 19, 1960

By virtue of Republic Act (RA) 2803, certain barrios in the municipality of Talibon were created.
By virtue of RA 2813, the barrio of Toril in the municipality of Maribojoc was created.
  • June 22, 1963

By virtue of RA 3364, certain barrios in the municipality of Sierra/Bullones were created.

  • June 19, 1965

By virtue of RA 4408, certain barrios in the municipality of Baclayon were created.

  • As of 2010

Bohol has three legislative districts. Listed below are the municipalities that comprise the districts:
1st District: Municipalities of Alburquerque, Antequera, Baclayon, Balilihan, Calape, Catigbian, Corella, Cortes, Dauis, Loon, Maribojoc, Panglao, Sikatuna, Tubigon and the city of Tagbilaran City.
2nd District: Municipalities of Bien Unido, Buenavista, Clarin, Dagohoy, Danao, Inabanga, Getafe (Jetafe), Pres. Carlos P. Garcia (Pitogo), Sagbayan (Borja), San Isidro, San Miguel, Talibon, Trinidad and Ubay.
3rd District: Municipalities of Alicia, Anda, Butuan, Bilar, Candijay, Carmen, Dimiao, Duero, Garcia Hernandez, Guindulman, Jagna, Lila, Loay, Loboc, Mabini, Pilar, Sevilla, Sierra Bullones, and Valencia.
