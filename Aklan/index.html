HISTORY

AKLAN

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998. 

Aklan is located at the northwestern part of Panay Island in the Western Visayas part of the Philippines.  The northern part of the province faces the Sibuyan Sea while the southern part faces Capiz province.

Aklan has a land area of 1,821.42 square kilometres and in 2007, it had a population of 495,122.

Following is a timeline 1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Aklan:

DATE

EVENT
  • February, 1213

The Bornean chieftains with about 1,000 followers arrived in Panay and bought the island from the Negrito inhabitants. They named the island Madia-as and divided them into three:

  • Hantik (Antique)
  • Aklan (Capiz and Aklan)
  • Irong-irong (Iloilo)
  • 1570

The island was comprised of a single province named Panay after it was left to the expedition of Martin de Goiti.
  • 1591

The island of Panay was subdivided into several encomiendas including Aclan, Batan (including Moguin, Dunblon and Baton), and perhaps others as well in the territory of Modern Aklan.

  • 1600

Panay was made a province with its capital at Arevalo after the demise of the encomienda system.
  • 1716

The politico-military province of Capiz which included the modern provinces of Aklan, Capiz, Romblon and the islands of Maestre de Ocampo was separated from the original province of Panay. Aklan remained part of Capiz up to the end of Spanish regime.
  • November 8, 1956

The province of Aklan was officially established with Kalibo as a capital by virtue of Republic Act 1414.
  • As of May 2013

Aklan constitutes a lone district that includes the municipalities of Altavas, Balete, Banga, Batan, Buruanga, Ibajay, Kalibo, Lezo, Libacao, Madalag, Makato, Malay, Malinao, Nabas, New Washington, Numancia and Tangalan.
