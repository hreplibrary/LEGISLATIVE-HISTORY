HISTORY

BATANES

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

Batanes is located in the northern coasts of Luzon. It is comprised of ten small islands namely Batan, Itbayat, Ibahos, Duquey, Siayan, Mabudis, Diogo, Sabtang, North and Y’Ami.

Batanes has a land area of 219.01 square kilometers. In 2007, it had a population of 15,974.

Following is a timeline1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Batanes:

DATE

EVENT
  • 1617

Dominicans attempted to evangelize the natives in Batan Island.
  • 1619

Dominican mission in Santa Ursula de Fuga was established.
  • 1722

Dominican mission in San Bartolome de Calayan was established.
  • 1784

Dominican missions in Basco and Ivana were established.
  • 1791

General Jose Basco led an expedition in the island and colonized Batanes. Five municipalities were established and made a district of Cagayan and placed under the alcalde mayor in Basco.
  • 1798

Dominican missions in Malintao were established.
  • 1834

“Islas Batanes” had just three pueblos – Santo Domingo de Basco, San Carlos de Mariato and San Jose de Ibana.
  • 1852-1855

 “Islas Batanes” became an independent province headed by a governor.

  • 1898

Batanes came under the control of the Revolutionary government under the Americans.
  • August 22, 1901

The province was annexed to Cagayan and a civil government was established.
  • 1903

The entire Batanes Island, including Magatao, Ibanag, Isbayat and Saptan were combined with Basco to form the single municipality of Basco.
The entire Babuyanes group of islands was combined to form the single municipality of Calayan.
  • 1907

Batanes was made a sub-province of Cagayan.
  • 1909

Batanes was separated from Cagayan as a “special” province with its capital located in Santo Domingo de Basco.
  • 1918

Batanes became a regular province.
  • June 16, 1955

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 1331, Sitio of San Vicente in the Municipality of Ivana, Batanes was converted into a barrio to be known as the Barrio of San Vicente.

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 1332, Sitio of Diptan in the Municipality of Basco, Batanes was converted into a barrio to be known as the Barrio of San Antonio de Padua.

By virtue of REPUBLIC ACT 1334, Sitios of Coral, Hago, Tuva, Diojango, Canalaan, Honaan and Dimnalamay in the municipality of Basco, Batanes were converted into a barrio to be known as the Barrio of San Joaquin.
  • As of 2013

Batanes constitutes a lone district that includes municipalities of Basco, Itbayat, Ivana, Mahatao, Sabtang, and Uyugan.

Sources:
