HISTORY

ALBAY

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

Albay is located in the southeastern portion of Luzon in the Bicol region.  It is located between the provinces of Camarines Sur on the north and Sorsogon on the south.

Albay has a land area of 2,565.77 square kilometres. In 2007, it had a population of 1,190,823.

Following is a timeline 1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Albay:

DATE

EVENT
  • 1569

Luis Enriquez de Guzman led an expedition from Panay to the islands of Masbate and Ticao, and to the region named Ibalon.
  • July, 1573

Juan de Salcedo, with 120 soldiers, explored Ibalon and founded the town Santiago de Libong.
  • 1616

The pueblo of Albay was organized and became the capital of the early “Partido de Ibalon”.
  • 1836

The province of Ibalon was formally renamed Albay by the Spanish.
  • 1846

Governor and Captain General Narciso de Claveria issued a decree separating Masbate, Ticao and Burias from Albay to form the comandancia of Masbate. Albay was divided into four districts: Iraya, Cordillera or Tabaco, Sorsogon and Catanduanes.
  • July 16, 1901
 By virtue of Act 169, the Island of Catanduanes was annexed to the Province of Albay
  • April 19, 1905
 By virtue of Act 1331, Catanduanes was established as sub-province of Albay.
  • October 31, 1906
 The number of municipalities was reduced from twenty-seven (27) to twenty-three (23),in the consolodation of certain municipalities through Act No. 1557.
  • October 1, 1907
 By virtue of Act 1731,the number of municipalities was reduced fro m twenty-three (23) to twenty-one (21),in the consolidation of the municipalities of Albay, Daraga, and Legaspi into one municipality.
  • March 10, 1917

The Province of Albay was created under Act 2711.

  • December 3, 1925
 By virtue of Act 3523, the municipality of Albay was changed to Legaspi
  • December 5, 1929

By virtue of Act No.  3617, the representative districts of the province of Albay were reorganized and a fourth district comprising the seven municipalities in the island of Catanduanes was created.
  • September 26, 1945

Catanduanes was established as regular province, separated from Albay through Commonwealth Act No. 687. At this point they now have three remaining District Representatives, retaining the existing First, Second and Third District of Albay.
  • 1945

Albay comprised 17 municipalities.
  • June 18, 1948

Legaspi became a chartered city through Republic Act 306
  • May 9, 1952
 Barrio San Pedro was created in the municipality of Tabaco through RA 692.
  • June 18, 1952
 Sitios of Anoling, Binandirahan, Cabraran, Ilawod, Mabugna, Magogon, Quinartilan, Solong, and Sumlang, all in the municipality of Camalig, were converted Barrios of the same municipality through RA 735.
  • June 8, 1954
 By virtue of RA 993,Legaspi and Daraga were recreated as a municipalities.
  • June 20, 1957
 By virtue of RA 1640, Sitio Canaway in the municipality of Maliipot was coverted into a Barrio.
  • June 12, 1959.
 Legazpi became a chartered city through Republic Act No. 2234,repealing all laws inconsistent with this the said Act
  • June 21, 1959

By virtue of RA 2524, Sitio Calbayog in the municipality of Maliipot was converted into a Barrio.

Also on the same date, certain barrios from the municipality of Daraga were created through RA 2538.
  • July 21, 1959

By virtue of RA 2505, the name of the municipality of Daraga was changed to municipality of Locsin.
Also on the same date,by virtue of RA 2520, the name of the municipality of Libog was changed to municipality of Sto. Domingo.

  • June 19, 1960
 By virtue of RA 2729, Sitio Magapo in the municipality of Tabaco was converted into a Barrio of the said municipality.Also on the same date, Barrio Madaat was created in the municipality of Daraga through RA 2829.
  • June 22, 1963

By virtue of RA 3817, Pio Duran was created as a municipality.
  • June 18, 1964
 By virtue of RA 3896, certain Barrios in the municipality of Libon were constituted.
  • June 17, 1967

By virtue of RA 4994, the name of the municipality of Locsin was changed to municipality of Daraga
  • June 21, 1969
 Boundary between the municipalities of Ligao and Pio Duran was fixed though RA 5863. Also on the same date, certain Barrios in the municipality of Guinobatan were created through RA 5778.
  • February 5, 1973
 By virtue of PD No. 125, certain provisions of Republic Act No. 2234 were amended
  • As of 2013

Albay constitutes three legislative districts. Listed below are the municipalities that comprise the districts:
1st District: Municipalities of Bacacay, Malilipot, Malinao, Santo Domingo, Tiwi, and the Tabaco City.
2nd District: Municipalities of Camalig, Daraga (Locsin), Manito, Rapu-Rapu, and Legazpi City.
3rd District: Municipalities of Guinobatan, Jovellar, Libon, Oas, Pio Duran, Polangui, Ligao City.
