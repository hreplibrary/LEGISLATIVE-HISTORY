HISTORY

EASTERN SAMAR

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

The province of Eastern Samar comprises the eastern portion of the island of Samar, located on the eastern side of the central Philippines.  It is bounded on the north and west by Northern and Western Samar, respectively.  The vast Pacific Ocean lies to the east, and the Leyte Gulf to the south.

Eastern Samar has a land area of 4,640.73 square kilometers. In 2007, it had a population 405,114.
