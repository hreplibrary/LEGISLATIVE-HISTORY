HISTORY

NEGROS OCCIDENTAL

The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

The province of Negros Occidental, known as the “sugar bowl” of the Philippines, occupies the western and northern parts of Negros Island in the central Visayan region of the archipelago.  A mountain range running through Negros Island’s center separates Negros Occidental from its sister province, Negros Oriental, to the east and south.  The rest of the province is bounded by the sea, with the Visayan Sea to the north, the Guimaras Strait (and Guimaras and Panay Islands beyond it) to the west, the Sulu Sea to the south, and the Tanon Strait to the east.
Negros Island was formerly called “Buglas”, but when the early Spaniards first explored it, they found many Negrito settlements.  Hence, they renamed it the island (Negros).

Although modern Occidental and Oriental Negros share a common island, their administration and early history was separate; the western half of the island originally having been administered from Iloilo (Arevalo) and the eastern half from Cebu.  Consequently, their histories are treated separately.

Following is a time line 1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Negros Occidental:

EVENT
  • 1521

Early missions were established in Binalbagan

expulsion of the Jesuits from the Philippines, the missions and parishes were turned over to the secular clergy

the Recollects took over their care

  • 1734

Earlier, Negros Occidental originally fell under the provincial jurisdiction of Iloilo (or Arevalo).  But in 1734, it was combined with the eastern half of Negros to form a single politico-military comandancia, or district.  The capital of Negros was originally at Ilog, but was moved almost immediately to Himamaylan
  • 1849

The capital of Negros was transferred to Bacolod
  • 1856

Negros Island’s status was elevated to that of a province, when a heavy influx of settlers (mainly from Panay) arrived to work in the newly flourishing sugar industry in Negros
  • 1890

Negros Province was split to form the two politico-military provinces of Negros Occidental and Negros Oriental.
  • November 1898

The Spanish surrendered. A provisional revolutionary government was set up immediately, with General Juan Araneta as governor.  It was soon decided, however, to re-unite the two provinces in Negros to form the new “Estado Cantonal De Negros,” or “Regional State of Negros”.
  • December 19, 1898

A general election was held on the island to elect representatives to the legislative assembly.
  • March 1899

arrival of U.S. troops
  • March 4, 1899

The capital of Bacolod was surrendered to the Americans
  • April 20, 1901

Civil government was restored. Negros was re-divided to form two separate provinces again, as under the last decade of the Spanish rule.  Negros Occidental capital remained at Bacolod, as before by virtue of Philippine Commission Act 119

By virtue of ACT 716, the 34 municipalities of the province Occidental Negros were reduced to 21.

  • 2010

Negros Occidental has 6 districts. The 1st district is composed of the municipalities of Calatrava, Salvador, Benedicto, and Toboso, and the Cities of Escalante and San Carlos. The 2nd district is composed of the municipalities of Manapla, Cadiz City, and Sagay City. The third district is composed of the municipalities of Enrique B. Magalona (Sarabia), and Murcia, and the Cities of Talisay, Victorias, and Silay. The 4th district is composed of the municipalities of Pontevedra, Pulupandan, San Enrique, Valladolid, Bago City, and La Carlota City. The 5th district is composed of the municipalities of Binalbagan, Hinigaran, Isabela, La Castellana, Moises Padilla (Magallon), and Himamaylan City. The 6th district is composed of the municipalities of Candoni, Cauayan, Hinoba-an (Asia), Ilog, Kabankalan City, and Sipalay City.
