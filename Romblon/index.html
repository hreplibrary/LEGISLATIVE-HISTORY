HISTORY

ROMBLON
The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

The province of Romblon is located nearly in the center of the Philippine Archipelago, and is comprised of the islands of Romblon, Tablas, Sibuyan, Banton, Simara, Carabao, Alad, Logbon, Cobrador, and a number of scattered islets.  It sits amid the Sibuyan Sea, with Marinduque to the north, Masbate to the east, Panay to the south, and Mindoro to the west.

Following is a timeline1,2 of events that led to the creation and development of the province of Romblon:

DATE

E V E N T
  • 1582

Spanish historian Loarca visited Romblon.  Tablas Island was then named Osigan.
  • 1645

The first Christian centers were established at Romblon and Banton then Banto, Sibuyan, Tablas, Simara and Sibali.
  • 1716

Romblon was annexed to Capiz from Arevalo or what is now known as Iloilo.
  • 1853

Romblon became a politico-military comandancia dependent on Capiz.
  • 1896

Most of the Spanish priests departed and left the parishes vacant for the next 32 years except fo Romblon, Badajoz (San Agustin) on Tablas Island and Cadiocan and San Fernando on Sibuyan Island.
  • 1898

Romblon came under the control of revolutionaries.
  • 1901

The Americans established a civil government for Romblon on March 16, 1901 covering the following municipalities:  Romblon, Tablas, Sibuyan, Banton, Maestre de Campo, and Simira Islands, theretofore comprising the old Spanish comandancia, and also the island of Carabao.  The capital was at Romblon
  • Nov 17, 1903

By virtue of Philippine Commission Act 994, the eleven municipalities of Romblon were reduced to six by consolidating Corcuera with Banton, Magallanes with Cajidiocan, Despujols with Odiongan, and Santa Fe with Looc.
  • 1907

Pursuant to Philippine Commission Act 1665, enacted July 2,1907, effective July 15, 1907, Romblon province was annexed as a sub-province to Capiz, with the exception of Maestre de Campo Island, which was annexed to Mindoro.
  • July 8, 1907
 By virtue of Act 1669, Province of Romblon was made as a sub-province of Capiz
  • 1918

On February 22, 1918, Romblon sub-province was again made an independent province by virtue of Philippine Legislature Act 2724, enacted December 7, 1917, as implemented by Executive Order No. 5 dated January 31, 1918 and effective February 22, 1918.
  • March 6, 1924
 By virtue of Act 313, the municipality of Concepcion was annexed to the province of Mindoro, segregated from the province of Romblon.
  • 1940

Four “special” municipalities were established.  The towns of Badajoz, Despujols, Odiongan and Looc were merged to form Tablas Municipality, Magdiwang, San Fernando and Cajidiocan formed Sibuyan municipality, Romblon Island became a special municipality, and Jones (Banton), Concepcion and Corcuera were fused to become Maghali municipality pursuant to Commonwealth Act 581, approved June 8, 1940. 

  • 1946

Republic Act 38, approved on October 1, 1946, restored Romblon to “regular” provincial status, as well as the towns as they existed before 1940. In addition, the municipality of Santa Fe was also created.
  • June 20, 1957
 By virtue of RA 1660,the municipality of Badajoz was changed to "San Agustin".
  • April 24, 1959
 By virtue of RA 2158,the municipality of Jones was changed to "Banton". 
  • June 18, 1961
 By virtue of RA 3358,the municipality of Despujols was changed to "San Andres".
  • June 18, 1961
 By virtue of RA 3423,Carabao Island was created as a municipality.
  • June 19, 1965
 By virtue of RA 4289,the municipality of Carabao Island was changed to "San Jose" 
  • June 15, 1968
 By virtue of RA 5317,Calatrava was created as a municipality
  • September 12, 1982
 By virtue of BP 234,Imelda was created as a municipality
  • April 17, 1988
 By virtue of 6651,the municipality of Imelda was changed to "Santa Maria".
  • As of 2013

Romblon province consists of the municipalities of Alcantara, Banton, Cajidiocan, Calatrava, Concepcion, Corcuera, Ferrol, Looc, Magdiwang, Odiongan, Romblon, San Agustin, San Andres, San Fernando, San Jose, Santa Fe, and Santa Maria (Imelda).
