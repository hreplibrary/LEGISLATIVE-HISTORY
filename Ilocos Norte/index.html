HISTORY

ILOCOS NORTE
The following text/information are lifted from the book of Lee W. Vance entitled Tracing your Philippine Ancestors which was published in Provo, Utah by Stevenson’s Genealogical Center in 1980 and Symbols of the State by the Department of Interior and Local Government of the Republic of the Philippines in 1998.

The province of Ilocos Norte can be found at the northwestern corner of Luzon Island in the northern part of the Philippines. It is surrounded on the east by the high mountains of Kalinga-Apayao and a small portion of Cagayan, on the southeast by Abra, and on the southwest by its sister province, Ilocos Sur.  It faces the waters of the South China Sea to the north and west. To date, most of Ilocos Norte shares much of its history with Ilocos Sur.

Laoag became the capital of the new province of Ilocos Norte after it was separated from Ilocos Sur. These two provinces have the same early history. (Velasco & Untivero, 1975 as cited in Vance, 1980)
  • August 20, 1901

Philippine Commission Act No. 207 was approved, proclaiming the establishment of a civil government in Ilocos Norte (Vance, 1980).
  • October 15, 1903

ACT 946 reduced THE 15 MUNICIPALITIES OF THE PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE TO 10.
  • October 15, 1903

Philippine Commission reduced the 15 municipalities of Ilocos Norte to 10 by

  • combining Nagpartian with Bangui;
  • Vintar with Bacarra;
  • San Nicolas with Laoag;
  • Solsona with Dingras; and Banna with Batac.
  • The remaining 10 towns have grown to the present total of 3 municipal districts, 19 municipalities and 1 city, Laoag City

By virtue of ACT NO. 2390, the names of the municipalities of Nagpartian and San Miguel, province of Ilocos Norte were changed.

  • As of 2013

Ilocos Norte has 2 districts. The 1st district is composed of Laoag City and the municipalities of Adams, Bacarra, Bangui, Burgos, Pagudpud, Pasuquin, Piddig, Sarrat, Vintar, carasi, and Dumalneg. The 2nd district is composed of the municipalities of badoc, Batac, Currimao, Dingras, Espiritu, Marcos, Nueva Era, Paoay, Pinili, San Nicolas, and Solsona.
