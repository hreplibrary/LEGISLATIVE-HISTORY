ABOUT THE PROJECT

The Legislative History in compact disc (CD) format is a product of the Office of the Secretary General in coordination with the Legislative Library Service (LLS) of the Congressional Library Bureau (CLB) under the Legislative Information Resource Management Department (LIRMD).

This electronic resource is an offshoot of another project, the Roster of Legislators.  The CD contains the roster of legislators from the very first Philippine Legislature to the 15th Congress by province/city with representation in the House.  It also includes a tabulation of all enacted measures relative to the province/city indicating the date of enactment, the title of the legislation and the source of information.  There is likewise, a time line of the creation/establishment of the province/city.

This CD would not have been possible without the hard work of the Library Team, the assistance of the Information Communications Technology Service (ICTS), and the support of the Printing & Reproduction Service (PRS), as well as the Procurement & Supply Management Service (PSPM).
